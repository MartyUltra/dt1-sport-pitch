Sport & T1D — software on top of existing CGM

Help people with type 1 diabetes who train hard anticipate glucose × workload × stress — without building a sensor, and without recommending insulin doses.

FACT (desk research)   No in-house sensor · scenario B (display / prediction / alerts) · not an AID

Problem

Exercise in T1D is clinically hard

  • Hypoglycaemia during activity
  • Delayed nocturnal hypos (up to ~12 hours after)
  • Opposite glucose responses in aerobic vs anaerobic work

Consumer “glucose wellness” excludes insulin users

  • Dexcom Stelo + Oura, Abbott Lingo: adults not on insulin
  • AID pumps at best offer Exercise / Temp Target modes — not rich sports coaching
  • Orphan niche on the consumer product side

Product vision

  • On top of already prescribed CGM / AID (Dexcom, Libre, Control-IQ, Omnipod 5…)
  • Aggregate glucose + recovery + stress (wearables)
  • Insights, trends, informational alerts — no dose recommendations
  • No sensor manufacturing (locked market; Diabeloop shows even software AID is commercially fragile)

Blockers Desk-research facts

Data access

  • Dexcom cloud API: retrospective EGV (~1 h US / ~3 h OUS) — real-time treatment decisions not allowed (FAQ)
  • DLA (Data Licensing Agreement) is not public = production gate
  • Abbott Libre: no public developer API — commercial partnership
  • HealthKit = technical path (G6/G7 push) ≠ green light for T1D coaching
  • Aggregators (BYOO) do not remove Dexcom’s delay

Regulatory & payer (France lens)

  • App alone → MDR MDSW (not IVDR)
  • Without dose advice: dual-track IIa (hypothesis) + IIb to budget; notified body ≥ IIa
  • AI Act (medical devices): obligations from 2 Aug 2028
  • Sports software outside day LPP codes → near-term payer = B2C
  • Do not price SaaS off hardware LPP tariffs

Forecasts / scenarios Hypotheses only

No invented TAM / SAM / CAGR. Scenarios inferred from the competitive landscape.

Hypothesis

Open space

If wellness stays closed to insulin users and AID stays limited to Exercise modes, soft T1D sports coaching (no dosing) may find an audience — to validate with interviews.

Hypothesis

Algorithm pressure

Diabeloop’s outcome (13 Oct 2026) plus partnerships like Sequel 2027 may reshuffle FR/US AID supply — watch, don’t depend on one hardware stack.

Hypothesis

Data gate

If Dexcom DLA / Abbott partnerships block cloud scenario B → fall back to on-device HealthKit or pause.

Hypothesis

Payer

Near term B2C; TLS/LPP only if clinical benefit and medical claims hold — long path, not the initial go.
