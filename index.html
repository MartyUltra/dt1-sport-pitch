Accessory concept — Soft Armor Band

Lifestyle band / short sleeve around an existing CGM (Dexcom or Libre) — not a new sensor. Dark, premium finish (graphite / midnight / matte black).

What it is

  • Soft textile sleeve with mesh window over the patch
  • TPU soft-touch rim · flatlock seams · light compression
  • Sizes S / M / L · remove sideways without peeling the CGM
  • Must not block Libre NFC scan or Dexcom Bluetooth

What it is not

  • Not a glucose sensor or dose advisor
  • No IP / waterproof medical claim without tests
  • Not hospital aesthetics — athletic wearable look
  • Concept only — field interviews & prototypes pending
